# Refund Policy

## Standard policy
- Full refund within 14 days of initial purchase (first-time subscribers)
- Prorated refund for annual plans cancelled within 30 days
- No refund after 30 days on annual plans (access continues until period end)
- Free trial: cancel before trial ends to avoid charge

## How to request
1. Account → Billing → Request refund
2. Or contact support with: email, transaction date, amount, reason

## Processing time
- 5–7 business days to original payment method
- PayPal may take up to 14 days

## When to escalate to P1
- User charged after cancelling before trial end
- Unauthorized charge with evidence of fraud
- Refund promised but not received after 10 business days

## Priority
- P1 for fraud/unauthorized charges
- P2 for standard refund requests within policy window
