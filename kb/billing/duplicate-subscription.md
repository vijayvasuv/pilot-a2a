# Duplicate Subscription Charge

## Symptoms
- Two charges on same day for same amount
- User upgraded plan but sees old + new charge
- Annual and monthly both charged

## Quick fixes
1. Check Billing → Subscriptions for duplicate active plans
2. Cancel the older/duplicate subscription tier
3. Refund for duplicate is processed within 5–7 business days if confirmed

## Common causes
- User subscribed on web and mobile separately
- Trial converted while another plan was active
- Family plan overlap

## When to escalate
- Duplicate charges persist after cancellation
- User charged 3+ times in one billing cycle
- Refund not received after 7 business days

## Priority
- P1 if total duplicate charges exceed $100
- P2 for single duplicate under $50
