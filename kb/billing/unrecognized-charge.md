# Unrecognized Charge on Card

## Symptoms
- Charge from company name user does not recognize
- Amount like $49.99 or $9.99 on statement
- User claims they never subscribed

## Quick fixes
1. Check if descriptor shows "APPNAME*SUBSCRIPTION" — may be our billing partner
2. Search email for receipt from last 30 days
3. Log into account → Billing → Payment history
4. Verify no family member shares the account

## Information to collect
- Exact amount and currency
- Date of charge
- Last 4 digits of card
- Statement descriptor text

## When to escalate
- User confirms no account exists with that email
- Charge appears fraudulent (wrong amount, repeated charges)
- User requests immediate refund for unauthorized charge

## Priority
- P1 for unauthorized/fraudulent charges over $50 or repeated charges
- P2 for charges under $50 with possible forgotten trial
